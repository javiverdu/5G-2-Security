# frame.protocols of the first frame at each point (icmp + tcp-http)

## icmp
P1-ue          raw:ip:icmp:data
P2-gnb         eth:ethertype:ip:udp:gtp:ip:icmp:data
P3-upf         eth:ethertype:ip:udp:gtp:ip:icmp:data
P4-upf         eth:ethertype:ip:icmp:data
P5-nginx       eth:ethertype:ip:icmp:data
